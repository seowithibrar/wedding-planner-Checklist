## How to Use This Printable Checklist

A paper checklist is easy to carry to venue tours, tastings and family meetings, and ticking boxes by hand is satisfying. Print this page from your browser, punch holes and keep it at the front of your wedding binder. Write the booking date and vendor name next to each task as you complete it.

**To print:** press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac), choose portrait layout, set the paper size to Letter or A4, and turn off browser headers and footers. Choose "Save as PDF" if you want a digital copy to print later.

- [ ] Hold menu tasting and confirm menu
- [ ] Plan ceremony with officiant

- [ ] Book marriage licence appointment
- [ ] Buy gifts for wedding party and parents

## 1 Month Before

- [ ] Follow up on missing RSVPs
- [ ] Finalise seating chart and place cards
- [ ] Final dress or suit fitting
- [ ] Confirm all vendor arrival times in writing
- [ ] Give final headcount to caterer and venue
- [ ] Write vows and speeches
- [ ] Prepare tip envelopes and final payments

## Final Week

- [ ] Share the day-of timeline with vendors and wedding party
- [ ] Pack the emergency kit
- [ ] Deliver decor, favours and signage to the venue
- [ ] Hold the rehearsal
- [ ] Pick up attire and rings
- [ ] Rest, hydrate and get an early night

## How to Organise a Wedding Binder

1. **Front pocket:** this printable checklist and the current month's priorities.
2. **Budget:** budget summary, payment log and receipts.
3. **Vendors:** contact sheet, contracts and quotes, one tab per vendor.
4. **Guests:** guest list summary, RSVP tracker and seating plan drafts.
5. **Design:** colour palette, swatches and inspiration. A [wedding moodboard](/blog/moodboard-layout) is a great first page for this section.
6. **Day-of:** the [wedding day-of timeline](/checklists/wedding-day-of-timeline-checklist), emergency contacts and shot list.

## Questions to Bring to Every Vendor Meeting

Print this list and keep it in the front pocket of your binder:

- Are you available on our date, and for how many hours?
- What exactly is included in this price?
- What extra charges could appear later?
- When are deposits and balances due?
- What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
- Who will be our contact on the wedding day?

## Day-Before Checklist

- [ ] Confirm transport times for the morning
- [ ] Lay out the full outfit, shoes, accessories and jewellery
- [ ] Charge phones, cameras and a power bank
- [ ] Put rings, vows and the marriage licence with the person responsible
- [ ] Hand tip envelopes and the vendor contact sheet to your coordinator
- [ ] Pack an overnight bag for the wedding night
- [ ] Set two alarms and plan a simple breakfast
- [ ] Go to bed early

## After the Wedding Checklist

- [ ] Return rented items and collect deposits
- [ ] Pay any remaining vendor balances
- [ ] Record gifts and send thank-you notes
- [ ] Leave reviews for vendors who did great work
- [ ] Order prints or an album once the gallery arrives
- [ ] Start name-change paperwork if you are changing your name
- [ ] Store the binder somewhere safe as a keepsake

## Tips for Using a Printed Checklist

- Write the date you completed each task, so you can see progress.
- Use one colour pen for booked items and another for tasks in progress.
- Keep a "questions for vendors" page at the back of the binder.
- Review the checklist every week at the same time.
- Make a fresh print each quarter so the list stays clean and readable.

If your timeline is shorter than 12 months, print the [6-month wedding planning checklist](/checklists/6-month-wedding-planning-checklist) or [3-month wedding planning checklist](/checklists/3-month-wedding-planning-checklist) instead.

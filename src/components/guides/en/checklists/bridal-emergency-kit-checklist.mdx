## Why You Need a Bridal Emergency Kit

Small problems happen at almost every wedding: a broken strap, a makeup smudge, a headache or a phone with no battery. None of them should slow down your day. A well-packed emergency kit lets the person beside you fix them in seconds, so you can keep enjoying the moment.

## Fashion and Dress Fixes

- Safety pins in several sizes
- Double-sided fashion tape
- Mini sewing kit with thread in the dress colour, white and black
- Small scissors
- Stain remover pen and white chalk for light fabric
- Lint roller
- Clear nail polish to stop tights from laddering
- Spare buttons and hooks
- Shoe grips, heel protectors and gel pads
- Spare tights or stockings
- Bustle ribbon instructions from your seamstress, with a photo

## Beauty and Hair

- Bobby pins and hair ties
- Travel-size hairspray
- Small comb or brush
- Blotting papers and pressed powder
- Lipstick or lip gloss you are wearing
- Concealer for quick touch-ups
- Cotton buds and makeup wipes
- Eye drops
- Spare false eyelashes and lash glue, if you wear them
- Mini perfume or body spray
- Deodorant
- Nail file and clear nail polish

## Health and Comfort

- Pain relief tablets
- Antacids or digestion tablets
- Allergy tablets
- Plasters and blister pads
- Personal medicines and inhaler, if needed
- Tissues
- Hand sanitiser
- Feminine hygiene products
- Breath mints or chewing gum
- Lip balm
- Sunscreen for outdoor weddings
- Insect repellent for evening outdoor events

## Food and Hydration

- Bottles of water with straws, to protect lipstick
- Small snacks such as nuts, granola bars or crackers
- Electrolyte sachets for hot days
- Mints for after eating

Many couples forget to eat on the wedding morning. Ask someone to make sure you eat breakfast and have a snack before the ceremony.

## Practical Items

- Phone charger and power bank
- Copy of the day-of timeline
- Vendor contact sheet with phone numbers
- Cash in small notes for tips or emergencies
- Pen and small notebook
- Envelopes for cards
- Umbrella for rain or sun
- Small fan for hot weather
- Pashmina or wrap for cool evenings
- Flat shoes for dancing
- Small tote bag for gifts and cards

## Ceremony Essentials

- Rings, given to the best man or a trusted person
- Marriage licence and any required documents
- Printed vows or speeches
- Payment envelopes for the officiant or vendors
- Readings, if guests are reading during the ceremony

## Partner and Groomsmen Kit

- Lint roller
- Collar stays and cufflinks
- Spare buttons
- Mini sewing kit
- Deodorant and travel cologne
- Comb and hair product
- Breath mints
- Spare socks
- Spare tie or pocket square
- Pain relief and plasters

## How to Pack the Kit

| Pouch | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Fashion pouch | Pins, tape, sewing kit, stain remover, lint roller |
| Beauty pouch | Makeup, hair items, blotting papers, perfume |
| Health pouch | Medicines, plasters, hygiene products |
| Snack pouch | Water, snacks, mints |
| Paper pouch | Timeline, contacts, cash, documents |

Use clear, labelled pouches so anyone can find items quickly. Pack everything into one small bag or box, and give a list of contents to the person who carries it.

## When to Pack

- **One week before:** buy anything missing.
- **Three to five days before:** pack the kit and label pouches.
- **The night before:** check the list and add your chargers.
- **Wedding morning:** add personal medicines, your lipstick and any last-minute items.

## Extras for Outdoor and Destination Weddings

- Sunscreen and after-sun lotion
- Insect repellent
- Rain ponchos or umbrellas
- Handheld fans and cooling wipes
- Blankets or wraps for cool evenings
- Travel adaptors and extra power banks

For destination weddings, also follow the [destination wedding checklist](/checklists/destination-wedding-checklist).

## Emergency Kit by Wedding Style

Different weddings need slightly different kits. Add these items to the core list:

| Wedding style | Extra items |
| --- | --- |
| Outdoor or garden | Sunscreen, insect repellent, fan, umbrella, heel protectors for grass |
| Winter or evening | Wrap or shawl, hand warmers, lip balm, moisturiser |
| Beach | Sand-proof bag, flat sandals, cooling wipes, hair ties for wind |
| Multi-day South Asian | Extra safety pins for dupattas, bindis, bangle box, mehndi aftercare oil, spare hair accessories |
| Destination | Travel adaptors, travel-size liquids, copies of documents, local currency |
| Long reception | Flat shoes, extra deodorant, plasters, snack for midnight |

## Quick Fixes for Common Wedding Day Problems

- **Torn hem:** use fashion tape or a quick tacking stitch with matching thread.
- **Broken strap:** secure it with a safety pin from the inside of the dress.
- **Makeup stain on fabric:** dab, do not rub, with a stain remover pen, then cover light marks with white chalk.
- **Shiny skin in photos:** press blotting paper on the face, then a light layer of powder.
- **Sore feet:** add gel pads early, before blisters start, and change into flat shoes for dancing.
- **Headache:** drink water, eat a snack and take pain relief if suitable for you.
- **Falling hairstyle:** cross two bobby pins in an X shape for a stronger hold, then spray.
- **Ring too tight from heat:** cool your hands with cold water and raise them for a minute.

## Mini Kit for Your Own Bag

Even if someone else carries the main kit, keep a small pouch with you: lipstick, blotting papers, two safety pins, two plasters, mints, tissues and a small amount of cash. It covers most small issues without needing to find anyone.

## Emergency Kit for the Wedding Party

Bridesmaids and other members of the wedding party benefit from a small shared kit as well: spare bobby pins, a few safety pins, blotting papers, plasters, mints, hair ties, a mini deodorant and a phone charger. Keep it with the person who manages the getting-ready room, so it is easy to find between photos.

## Common Emergency Kit Mistakes

1. **Packing it too late.** Last-minute shopping causes stress on wedding week.
2. **Giving it to someone who will not be nearby.** Choose someone who stays close.
3. **No colour-matched thread.** Bring thread that matches your dress.
4. **Forgetting food and water.** Long days without food lead to headaches.
5. **No charger.** Phones run out of battery fast on a busy day.

## Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how WeddingPlanningChecklists.org ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit https://www.weddingplanningchecklists.org/ and use our free wedding planning checklists, guides and tools. By using the website, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the website.

## The Short Version

- You can read every guide and use every tool without creating an account.
- The planning data you type into our tools, such as guest names, budgets, tasks and calendar entries, is stored **in your own browser**, not on our servers.
- We use Google Analytics to understand how the website is used, and Google AdSense to show advertising that keeps the website free.
- These Google services use cookies and similar technologies. You can control them through your browser and through Google's own settings.
- We do not sell or rent your personal information.

## Information We Collect

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, some technical information is collected automatically by our hosting provider and by the analytics and advertising services described below. This can include:

- Your IP address, which may be shortened or anonymised by the service that processes it.
- Your browser type, device type, operating system and screen size.
- The pages you visit, how long you stay and the website or search engine that referred you.
- The approximate location derived from your IP address, usually at country or city level.
- The date and time of your visit and basic diagnostic information, such as errors.

We use this information in aggregate to understand which guides are useful, fix technical problems and improve the website.

### Information you type into our tools

Our planning tools, including the Budget Calculator, Guest List Manager, Wedding Checklist Generator, Wedding Planning Calendar, Wedding Countdown and Wedding Planning Dashboard, save what you enter using your browser's local storage. This means:

- The data stays on the device and browser you used.
- We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us.
- Clearing your browser data, or using private or incognito mode, will remove it.
- If you use a shared or public computer, remember to clear your browser data when you finish.

Some tools let you export your data as a CSV file. The file is created in your browser and downloaded directly to your device.

### Information you choose to send us

Where the website offers a contact form or newsletter sign-up, any information you decide to submit, such as your name, email address and message, is used only for the purpose you submitted it for: to reply to your message or to send the newsletter you asked for. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a website remember information on your device. We and our partners use them for the following purposes:

| Type | Purpose | Set by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essential storage | Remembering the data you enter into our planning tools and your language choice | WeddingPlanningChecklists.org (your browser's local storage) |
| Analytics | Measuring visits, popular pages and traffic sources | Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager |
| Advertising | Showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad performance and, where permitted, showing ads based on your interests | Google AdSense and its advertising partners |
| Fonts | Loading the typefaces used on the website | Google Fonts |

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage will stop our tools from remembering your entries between visits.

## Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as the pages you view and how you arrived at the website, and it reports this to us in aggregate form. We do not use Google Analytics to identify individual visitors.

You can learn how Google uses this information on the page "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" in Google's policies, and you can opt out of Google Analytics across websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

## Google AdSense and Advertising

We display advertising through Google AdSense so that our checklists and tools can remain free. In connection with this:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
- In regions where the law requires consent before personalised advertising cookies are used, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message may be shown on your first visit. You can change the choice you made at any time.

We do not control the cookies used by advertisers, and an advertisement appearing on the website is not an endorsement of the product or service advertised.

## How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- Operate the website and keep it secure.
- Understand which content and tools are helpful, so we can improve them.
- Show advertising that funds the website.
- Reply to messages you send us.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use the planning data you enter into our tools for any of these purposes, because that data stays in your browser.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only:

- With service providers that help us run the website, such as our hosting provider, Google Analytics and Google AdSense, under their own privacy terms.
- When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our visitors and the website.
- In connection with a merger, sale or transfer of the website, in which case this policy would continue to apply to information collected before the transfer.

## How Long We Keep Information

Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of our Google Analytics property, after which it is deleted automatically. Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping. Data stored in your browser stays until you clear it.

## Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent you have given. You can also:

- Clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser.
- Opt out of personalised ads through Google's Ads Settings.
- Opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

To make a request about information we hold, please reach us through our [Contact page](/contact). We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

## Children's Privacy

The website is intended for adults planning a wedding. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

## External Links

Our guides link to other websites, such as vendors, retailers and social networks. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the website, including serving every page over a secure HTTPS connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or the law change. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Please check this page from time to time.

## How to Clear Your Planning Data

To remove the information our tools have saved in your browser, open your browser settings, find the section for cookies and site data, search for weddingplanningchecklists.org and choose to delete its stored data. You can also clear all browsing data, which removes data stored by every website. Export or print anything you want to keep before you do this, because deleted browser data cannot be recovered.

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. There is no single industry standard for responding to these signals. You can still control analytics and advertising cookies using your browser settings and the Google opt-out tools described above.

## International Visitors

The website is available worldwide, and the service providers we use, such as Google, may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. These providers use their own safeguards for international transfers of data.

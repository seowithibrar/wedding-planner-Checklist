## How We Can Help

We read every message sent through this page. To help us reply quickly and usefully, here is what we can and cannot help with.

### We are happy to hear from you about

- **Corrections.** An error in a checklist, an outdated cost, a broken link or a tool that is not working as expected.
- **Guide suggestions.** A wedding planning topic, culture, budget level or timeline you would like us to cover.
- **Tool feedback.** Ideas to make the Budget Calculator, Guest List Manager, Checklist Generator, Planning Calendar, Countdown or Dashboard more useful.
- **Translation feedback.** Wording in our Spanish pages that reads unnaturally or could be clearer.
- **Media and partnership enquiries.** Press requests, interview requests or permission to reproduce our content.
- **Privacy requests.** Questions about the information described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

### What we cannot help with

- **Personal wedding planning or coordination.** We are a free planning resource, not a planning agency, so we cannot plan or manage individual weddings.
- **Vendor bookings or disputes.** We do not book, represent or resolve disputes with venues, vendors or retailers. Contact the vendor directly and refer to your contract.
- **Legal, tax or financial advice.** For marriage licence rules, contracts or financial decisions, please speak to the relevant local authority or a qualified professional.
- **Paid placements.** We do not accept payment to feature or recommend vendors, venues or products in our editorial content.

## How to Write a Message We Can Act On

A clear message gets a faster and more useful reply. When you write to us, please include:

1. **The page address.** Copy the link from your browser's address bar if your message is about a specific page or tool.
2. **What you expected and what happened.** For tool problems, tell us what you clicked and what you saw.
3. **Your device and browser.** For example, "iPhone, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome". This helps us reproduce technical issues.
4. **Your suggested change.** For corrections, tell us what you believe the correct information is and, if possible, where it comes from.

Please do not include sensitive personal information, such as payment details, identity documents or your guests' private contact details.

## Reporting a Problem With a Planning Tool

Our tools save your information in your own browser rather than on our servers. Before reporting a problem, try these steps, which solve most issues:

- **Data disappeared?** Make sure you are using the same browser and device as before, and that you are not in private or incognito mode, which deletes stored data when the window closes.
- **A button does nothing?** Refresh the page and try again. If you use an ad blocker or strict privacy extension, try pausing it for our website, as some extensions block scripts the tools need.
- **Numbers look wrong?** Check that amounts are entered as numbers without currency symbols or commas, then refresh.
- **Printing looks odd?** Use your browser's print preview and select "Fit to page" or a smaller scale.

If the problem continues, send us the details listed above and we will look into it.

## Suggesting a New Guide

We decide what to write next based on what couples need. The most helpful suggestions tell us:

- The situation you are planning for, such as a winter wedding, a two-day celebration, a blended family or a very small budget.
- The question you could not find a clear answer to.
- What would make the guide useful to you, such as a printable checklist, a budget breakdown or a sample timeline.

We cannot promise to publish every suggestion, but recurring requests go to the top of our list.

## Response Times

We aim to reply to most messages within a few business days. Corrections and technical problems are prioritised. During busy periods, such as the start of the year when many couples begin planning, replies may take a little longer. We may not reply to messages that are promotional, automated or unrelated to wedding planning.

## Privacy of Your Message

Anything you send through this page is used only to read and respond to your message, and is never sold or shared for marketing. You can ask us to delete your message at any time. For full details, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## Accessibility Feedback

We want every checklist and tool to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom or high-contrast settings. If any part of the website is difficult to read or use, tell us which page you were on, what assistive technology or setting you use, and what got in the way. Accessibility reports are treated with the same priority as broken tools.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Contacting Us

### Can you recommend a vendor or venue in my city?

We do not recommend individual vendors, because we cannot inspect or vouch for them, and we do not accept payment for recommendations. Instead, use our guide on [how to choose the perfect wedding venue](/blog/how-to-choose-wedding-venue) and the [wedding vendor booking checklist](/checklists/wedding-vendor-booking-checklist), which list the questions to ask and the warning signs to look out for.

### Can you check my wedding budget or timeline?

We cannot review individual plans, but the free tools on the website do much of this for you. The [Budget Calculator](/tools/budget-calculator) shows whether your category split is realistic, and the [Wedding Checklist Generator](/tools/wedding-checklist-generator) builds a dated task list from your wedding date.

### I found a mistake. How quickly will it be fixed?

Factual errors, wrong figures and broken links are usually corrected soon after we confirm them. Larger changes, such as rewriting a section, are scheduled into our regular content updates. The "Updated" date on the page changes when a meaningful correction is made.

### Can I republish one of your checklists on my website or blog?

You are welcome to link to any page and quote a short excerpt with credit. To reproduce a full checklist, table or graphic, please ask for written permission first and explain where it will appear.

### Do you offer printable versions of your checklists?

Most checklists can be printed directly from your browser. Use the print option in your browser menu and choose "Save as PDF" to keep a digital copy. Our [printable wedding planning checklist](/checklists/printable-wedding-planning-checklist) is designed specifically for paper.

### Can I write a guest article for the website?

We do not currently accept guest posts or sponsored articles. All guides are written and edited for this website so we can keep a consistent standard and structure.

### Will you share my email address with anyone?

No. Contact details you send us are used only to reply to you. They are not sold, rented or added to marketing lists without your permission.

## Contacting Us About an Advertisement

Ads on the website are served by Google AdSense and chosen automatically. We do not select individual advertisers. If you see an advertisement that seems misleading, offensive or inappropriate, please tell us the page you saw it on and, if possible, the advertiser's name or the address it linked to. You can also use the "AdChoices" or "Why this ad?" icon on the ad itself to report it directly to Google or to stop seeing similar ads.

## Contacting Us About Your Data

Because our planning tools store your entries in your own browser, we cannot recover, view or delete them for you. To remove that data yourself, clear your browser's site data for WeddingPlanningChecklists.org. For any other information request, such as deleting a message you sent us, write to us through this page and describe your request clearly.

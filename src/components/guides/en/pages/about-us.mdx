## Why WeddingPlanningChecklists.org Exists

Planning a wedding is a project with hundreds of moving parts, a fixed deadline and, for most couples, no previous experience. Many planning resources either stay too general to act on or bury useful advice under sales pitches. We built WeddingPlanningChecklists.org to do one thing well: turn wedding planning into clear, ordered steps that anyone can follow, for free.

Every checklist, guide and tool on this website is designed to answer three practical questions:

1. **What do we need to do?**
2. **When do we need to do it?**
3. **How much is it likely to cost, and where can we save?**

## How We Create Our Content

We follow the same process for every guide we publish.

### 1. Start with the question couples are actually asking

Every article starts from a real planning problem: how far ahead to book a photographer, how to split a $30,000 budget, or what a three-day Pakistani wedding needs. Each section of a guide has to answer a question a couple would ask. If a section does not help someone make a decision or take an action, it does not belong.

### 2. Build the plan in the order it happens

Planning advice is only useful when it is in the right order. We structure checklists around booking windows, because the venue, photographer and caterer usually need to be secured long before stationery, favours or seating charts.

### 3. Be specific

We prefer concrete guidance over vague tips. That means sample budget splits, questions to ask on a venue tour, typical lead times, warning signs in vendor contracts and examples of what to delegate. When costs are shown, they are presented as typical ranges and examples, because real prices vary by location, season and guest count.

### 4. Check and update

Wedding planning changes: prices rise, trends shift and new tools appear. We review our guides and update them when something meaningful changes. Each page shows the date it was last updated.

### 5. Write for people first

Our guides are written to be read by real couples, not stuffed with keywords. We use plain language, short sections, tables for comparisons and checklists you can tick off.

## Our Editorial Standards

- **Helpful first.** Content exists to help you plan, not to fill space.
- **Original.** Our checklists and guides are written for this website. We do not copy other publishers' work.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** Where an answer depends on your location, budget or culture, we say so rather than pretend there is one correct answer.
- **No pay-for-praise.** We do not accept payment to recommend a vendor, venue or product in our editorial content.

## Where to Start

If you are not sure where to begin, these three steps work for almost every couple:

1. **Pick the timeline that matches your engagement.** Choose the checklist closest to the time you have left, or read [how to plan a wedding timeline](/blog/how-to-plan-a-wedding-timeline) if you are still deciding on a date.
2. **Set your budget.** Enter your total into the [Budget Calculator](/tools/budget-calculator) before you speak to vendors, so you know what each category can afford.
3. **Draft the guest list.** Your guest count drives venue size, catering and invitations. Our [guide to organising your guest list and seating plan](/blog/perfect-guest-list-guide) shows you how.

For a complete overview of the whole process, read [the ultimate wedding planning checklists guide](/blog/the-ultimate-wedding-planning-checklists-guide-for-a-stress-free-wedding).

## What Makes Our Checklists Different

A good checklist does more than list tasks. Ours are built around a few principles that we apply to every guide:

- **Ordered by deadline, not by topic.** Tasks appear when you need to act on them, so you are never booking a photographer after the best ones are gone.
- **Built around decisions.** Instead of just saying "choose a caterer", we explain what to compare, what to ask and what to watch for in the contract.
- **Realistic about money.** We show how budgets are typically split and where hidden costs such as service charges, taxes and overtime fees tend to appear.
- **Flexible.** Every plan can be shortened, stretched or adapted for cultural traditions, destination weddings and small celebrations.
- **Connected to tools.** Checklists link to the calculator, guest list manager and calendar, so you can act on the advice straight away.

## How We Choose What to Cover

We decide which guides to write based on the questions couples ask most often, the planning stages where people tend to get stuck, and gaps where existing advice is unclear or incomplete. That is why our library covers both the essentials, such as timelines and budgets, and the details that matter to specific weddings, such as multi-day cultural celebrations and seasonal planning.

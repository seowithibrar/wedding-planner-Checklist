## Can You Plan a Wedding in 3 Months?

Yes, you can plan a wedding in 3 months. Ninety days is enough for a beautiful, well-run wedding if you decide quickly, keep the guest list manageable, and book your venue and photographer in the first week.

What changes is the method, not the quality. A 12-month plan lets you compare and wait. A 3 month wedding planning checklist asks you to choose, book, and move on. Popular Saturday dates at well-known venues may already be gone, so flexibility on the day of the week or the venue type matters more than any other single decision.

If you have more time, our [6-month wedding planning checklist](/checklists/6-month-wedding-planning-checklist) gives you room to compare vendors. If you have a lot more time, the [12-month wedding planning checklist](/checklists/12-month-wedding-planning-checklist) spreads the same work across a full year.

## How to Use This 3 Month Wedding Planning Checklist

Start by counting backward from your wedding date. Put the date into the [Wedding Countdown](/tools/wedding-countdown) so you can see exactly how many weeks and Saturdays you have left. Then work through this 3 month wedding planning checklist in order.

Three habits make the plan work:

- Contact several vendors at once instead of one at a time, because waiting for replies is the slowest part of a short timeline.
- Give every vendor a reply deadline of about two days, then move on to the next option.
- Log each deposit, contract, and due date the moment it happens.

You can tick tasks off live in the [Wedding Planning Dashboard](/tools/wedding-planning-dashboard), or generate a version tailored to your wedding with the [Wedding Checklist Generator](/tools/wedding-checklist-generator).

## The First 72 Hours: Decisions That Unlock Everything Else

Before you contact a single vendor or tick off anything else on this 3 month wedding planning checklist, settle four things. Everything else depends on them.

<img src="/wedding-planning-first-72-hours-decisions.svg" alt="Notebook with four early wedding planning decisions for a 3 month timeline" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

- **Total budget.** Write one number down, including any family contribution and any conditions attached to it. The [Budget Calculator](/tools/budget-calculator) shows how that number splits across categories.
- **Guest count.** Venue size, catering, invitations, and seating all follow from it. A smaller list is the strongest lever on a short timeline.
- **Date flexibility.** Decide whether a weekday, a Friday, or a Sunday is acceptable. Off-peak dates open far more options.
- **Your top three priorities.** Maybe it is the food, the photography, and the venue. Spend your effort and money there first.

## Weeks 12 to 10: Book the Essentials First

The opening weeks of a 3 month wedding planning checklist are about booking. These are the vendors who run out of availability fastest, so they come first. Do not do them one after another. Start all of them in the same week.

### Venue and date

- Ask venues about weekday, Friday, and Sunday availability before you give up on a favorite.
- Look beyond traditional wedding venues. Restaurants with private rooms, gardens, community halls, and private homes are often easier to book. Our guide on [how to choose the perfect wedding venue](/blog/how-to-choose-wedding-venue) lists the questions to ask on every tour.
- Venues that include catering, tables, and chairs solve several bookings in one contract.
- Moving your date by one or two weeks can open options that a fixed date never will.

### Photographer and videographer

- Contact at least five photographers on the same day and ask one direct question: are you free on my date?
- Check full galleries, not just highlight reels.
- If your first choices are booked, ask your venue for referrals.

### Caterer, officiant, and planner

- Confirm what your venue's catering includes before hiring anyone else.
- Book your officiant or celebrant now and ask what paperwork they need from you.
- Consider a day-of coordinator even if you plan everything else yourself. On a short timeline, someone other than you or your family should run the vendors on the day.
- Use the [Vendor Booking Order & Contract Checklist](/checklists/wedding-vendor-booking-checklist) to confirm what each contract should say before you sign.

## Weeks 9 to 7: Style, Guests, and Look

### Attire

- Choose ready-to-wear, off-the-rack, sample-sale, or rental options. Custom orders rarely fit a 90-day window.
- Book the tailor at the same appointment you buy the outfit. Alterations commonly take several weeks, so the earlier the first fitting, the safer you are.
- Keep the wedding party simple: one color family, with each person choosing an in-stock style.

### Invitations and guests

- Skip save-the-dates. Go straight to invitations, and consider digital ones because they arrive instantly.
- Build a simple wedding website with the date, location, schedule, and travel details, then share the link by text and call.
- Track every reply in the [Guest List Manager](/tools/guest-list-manager). The [Guest List & RSVP Master Checklist](/checklists/wedding-guest-list-organizer) covers meal choices, plus-ones, and follow-ups.
- Set an RSVP deadline roughly four to five weeks before the wedding.

### Hair, makeup, and flowers

- Book hair and makeup artists early and schedule one trial.
- Keep florals simple and seasonal. Reusing ceremony flowers at the reception saves money and setup time. The [Decor & Floral Checklist](/checklists/wedding-decor-flowers-checklist) helps you decide what is worth paying for.

## Weeks 6 to 4: Paperwork, Details, and Logistics

- **Marriage license.** Rules differ by country, state, and region. Check the waiting period, how long the license stays valid, and whether both of you must appear in person.
- **Rings.** Buy in-stock bands if engraving or sizing could delay delivery. You can often engrave later.
- **Menu.** Finalize choices, dietary needs, and drink quantities with your caterer.
- **Seating.** Start a first draft even before every reply is in.
- **Timeline.** Build the first version of your wedding day schedule with the [Day-of Timeline Generator](/tools/timeline-generator). Start at the ceremony time, work backward for getting ready, and forward for dinner and dancing.
- **Contracts.** Confirm every vendor has a signed contract and a recorded deposit. A verbal yes is not a booking.

## Weeks 3 to 2: Confirm Everything

This stretch of the 3 month wedding planning checklist is about confirming, not deciding.

- Call each vendor to confirm date, arrival time, address, parking, and an emergency phone number.
- Send your final headcount to the caterer and venue.
- Attend the last dress or suit fitting and bring the shoes you will wear.
- Share the finished day-of timeline with your vendors and wedding party, with a short written brief for each person.
- Prepare tip envelopes and name one trusted person to hand them out.
- Check passports and travel documents if you are leaving for a honeymoon.

## The Final Week: Rehearse, Pack, and Rest

The last page of your 3 month wedding planning checklist is short on purpose.

- Attend the rehearsal if you are having one, and walk through the ceremony order.
- Pack a [bridal emergency kit](/checklists/bridal-emergency-kit-checklist) with the small fixes that save a morning.
- Deliver décor, guest book, and personal items to the venue ahead of time.
- Confirm transport home and wedding night accommodation.
- Sleep. The plan is built, and the people you briefed can carry it.

The [Day-Of Schedule & Timeline Checklist](/checklists/wedding-day-of-timeline-checklist) gives you a final pass for the day itself.

## What to Book First, Simplify, or Skip

A 3 month wedding planning checklist is as much about choosing what not to do as what to do. Use this table to cut tasks without cutting the guest experience.

| Item | On a 3-month timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Venue and photographer | Book in week one |
| Live band | Often booked far ahead, so consider a DJ or a curated playlist |
| Custom wedding dress | Choose off-the-rack, sample-sale, or rental instead |
| Save-the-dates | Skip them and send invitations early |
| Custom printed stationery | Use digital invitations or a simple print option |
| Engagement photos | Optional, and usually not worth the time |
| Wedding favors | Skip or keep very simple |
| Long vendor comparisons | Contact a few, book the first one that fits |

## 90 Day Wedding Planning Checklist for a Small Budget

Planning a wedding in 3 months with a small budget uses the same 3 month wedding planning checklist, but it works best when you protect your priorities and trim everything else. A 90 day wedding planning checklist on a smaller budget leans on four ideas:

- Choose a weekday or Sunday date, which often costs less at the same venue.
- Keep the guest list small. Every guest adds catering, seating, and stationery cost.
- Pick an all-inclusive venue so you are not paying separately for tables, chairs, and setup.
- Spend on two or three priorities and keep the rest simple.

## Planning a Wedding in 2 Months or 6 Months?

With 2 months left, keep every step in this list but compress it. Book the venue and photographer in the first three days, send digital invitations immediately, and use ready-to-wear attire only. Drop anything optional.

With 6 months left, you can slow down. Use the [6-month wedding planning checklist](/checklists/6-month-wedding-planning-checklist), which allows more vendor comparison, and add a proper save-the-date.

## Mistakes to Avoid With Your 3 Month Wedding Planning Checklist

- **Researching too long.** The first month is for booking, not browsing.
- **Reopening decisions.** Once you have chosen a vendor, stop comparing.
- **Ignoring family expectations.** Agree the guest list and contributions early, before deposits are paid.
- **Skipping contracts.** Put every booking in writing, even for friends and family.
- **Doing everything yourself.** Hand out tasks. A written brief for each helper works better than a verbal one.
- **Forgetting the paperwork.** Check marriage license rules in week one and again in week six.

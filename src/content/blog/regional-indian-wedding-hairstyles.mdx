## Regional Indian Wedding Hairstyles: A Cultural Tour

India's rich diversity is reflected in its stunning bridal hairstyles. From South Indian long temple braids to North Indian velvet-framed judas, each region possesses distinct bridal hair traditions.

### Regional Hairstyles Explored

#### 1. South Indian Temple Braids (Poojadai)
South Indian brides wear long, waist-length braids decorated with temple gold coins (billai), fresh jasmine strands, and elaborate floral nets.

#### 2. North Indian Juda & Dupatta Setting
North Indian brides pair traditional low buns with rich Zardozi dupattas, maang tikkas, and matha pattis.

#### 3. Bengali Bridal Bun & Mukut
Bengali brides wear a neat center-parted bun designed to anchor the traditional white Mukut tiara and red-bordered saree pallu.

#### 4. Maharashtrian Ambada
The classic Maharashtrian Ambada bun is adorned with pearl Mundavalya hanging across the forehead and fresh floral wreaths.

## 50+ Best Hairstyles for Wedding

Choosing the right bridal hairstyle is all about framing your face, supporting your veil or dupatta, and staying comfortable through hours of dancing.

<PlannerQuote author="Marcus Vance" role="Celebrity Bridal Stylist">
Your hairstyle should complement your gown's neckline, not compete with it. If your dress has intricate back detailing, opt for an updo to showcase it.
</PlannerQuote>

<CalloutBox type="pro" title="Hair Trial Preparation">
Bring your veil, headpiece, earrings, and photos of your dress neckline to your hair trial 60-90 days before your wedding.
</CalloutBox>

### Top Bridal Hairstyle Trends

#### 1. Classic Low Bun
Sleek, elegant, and timeless. Low buns provide a secure foundation for heavy veils and tiaras while keeping hair neatly secured all night.

#### 2. Hollywood Glam Waves
Dramatic side-parted glossy waves add red-carpet glamour. Best paired with strapless or sweetheart necklines.

#### 3. Textured Floral Braids
Woven with fresh jasmine, baby's breath, or delicate pearl pins for bohemian or traditional ceremony aesthetics.

| Hairstyle | Best Gown Neckline | Weather Resistance | Veil Support |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Classic Low Bun | High Neck, Backless, V-Neck | High | Excellent |
| Hollywood Waves | Strapless, Sweetheart | Medium | Moderate |
| Textured Braid | Off-the-Shoulder, Boho | High | Good |
| High Updo | Illusion Lace, Halter | High | Excellent |

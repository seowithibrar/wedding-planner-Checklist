## 50+ Best Hairstyles for Wedding

Choosing the right bridal hairstyle is all about framing your face, supporting your veil or dupatta, and staying comfortable through hours of dancing.

### Popular Bridal Hairstyles
- **Classic Low Bun**: Sleek, elegant, and perfectly holds heavy veils.
- **Hollywood Glam Waves**: Dramatic side-parted glossy waves.
- **Textured Floral Braids**: Woven with fresh jasmine or delicate pearl pins.

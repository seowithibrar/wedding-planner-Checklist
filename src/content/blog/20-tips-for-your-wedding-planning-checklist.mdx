## 20 Expert Tips for Your Wedding Planning Checklist

Streamline your wedding planning journey with these 20 proven insider tips from professional wedding planners.

### Top 5 Planning Rules
1. **Establish Budget First**: Never tour venues without knowing your exact financial boundaries.
2. **Read Contracts Carefully**: Verify cancellation policies, overtime fees, and rain backup terms.
3. **Buffer Time**: Add 15 extra minutes between getting ready, transit, and photo sessions.
4. **Feed Your Vendors**: Include hot meals for your photographer, videographer, DJ, and coordinator in your catering count.
5. **Savor the Process**: Schedule non-wedding date nights with your partner to stay connected.

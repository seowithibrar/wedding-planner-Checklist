## Mastering Your Wedding Guest List & Seating Charts

Creating your guest list is often cited as the most stressful part of wedding planning. Clear rules and strategic seating charts keep family politics at bay.

### Guest List Rules
1. **Capacity Limit**: Never exceed your venue's maximum fire capacity.
2. **Consistent Plus-One Policy**: Apply rules equally across all friend and family groups.
3. **Seating Harmony**: Mix friendly social groups while keeping conflicting relatives separated.

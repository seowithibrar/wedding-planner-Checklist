## Indian Wedding Hairstyles: The Complete 2026 Guide

Your wedding hairstyle is far more than a beauty choice—it connects centuries of bridal tradition, frames your jewelry, and supports your bridal dupatta throughout long rituals.

### Hairstyles by Wedding Function

#### 1. Haldi & Mehendi
Lightweight, playful hairstyles work best for pre-wedding functions. Loose floral braids, side-swept curls, or half-up styles decorated with marigolds and baby's breath create a breezy, festive look.

#### 2. Sangeet Night
Sangeet calls for high drama and movement. Voluminous open Hollywood waves, texturedfishtail braids, or chic messier buns withstand energetic dance performances.

#### 3. Main Wedding Ceremony
For the main pheras, structure and security are essential. Classic low judas padded with hair donuts ensure heavy maang tikkas, matha pattis, and duppattas stay anchored for hours.

#### 4. Reception Night
Transition to modern elegance with a sleek high bun, a polished ponytail, or cascading glam curls paired with statement earrings.

## The Ultimate 14-Month Wedding Planning Timeline

Planning your wedding across 14 months allows you to maintain steady progress without overwhelming your daily schedule.

### Month 14-12: The Foundation
- Define budget limits and create your master financial sheet.
- Draft guest list count to filter appropriate venues.
- Book venue and secure photographer.

### Month 11-8: Major Vendor Lock
- Taste catering menus and select bar packages.
- Contract florist, lighting, and DJ entertainment.
- Order custom bridal gown or wedding suits.

### Month 7-4: Fine Details & Invitations
- Mail formal invitation suites.
- Schedule beauty trial runs.
- Create seating charts and table arrangements.

## The Ultimate 14-Month Wedding Planning Timeline

Planning your wedding across 14 months allows you to maintain steady progress without overwhelming your daily schedule.

<PlannerQuote author="Elena Rostova" role="Event Logistics Director">
14 months gives you the exact sweet spot: enough time to secure prime vendors without keeping your life on hold for two full years.
</PlannerQuote>

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 14-12)

<CalloutBox type="tip" title="Guest Count First">
Determine your guest list count before visiting venues—capacity dictates which spaces will work comfortably for your celebration.
</CalloutBox>

- **Month 14**: Define budget limits and create your master financial sheet.
- **Month 13**: Draft guest list count to filter appropriate venues.
- **Month 12**: Book venue and secure photographer.

| Phase | Months | Primary Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Foundation | 14 - 12 | Budget, Venue, Photo & Video |
| Core Booking | 11 - 8 | Caterer, Attire, Decor, Music |
| Design & Details | 7 - 4 | Invitations, Beauty Trial, Floral Mockups |
| Final Sprint | 3 - 0 | Seating Chart, Final Headcount, Tip Envelopes |

### Phase 2: Core Booking (Months 11-8)

<CalloutBox type="pro" title="Attire Lead Times">
Most designer bridal gowns require 4-6 months for tailoring and delivery. Order by Month 10 at the latest!
</CalloutBox>

- **Month 11**: Taste catering menus and select bar packages.
- **Month 10**: Contract florist, lighting, and DJ entertainment.
- **Month 9**: Order custom bridal gown or wedding suits.
- **Month 8**: Reserve hotel room blocks for out-of-town guests.

### Phase 3: Fine Details & Invitations (Months 7-4)

- **Month 7**: Purchase wedding bands and schedule beauty trial runs.
- **Month 5**: Approve cake design and centerpiece floral mockups.
- **Month 4**: Mail formal invitation suites.

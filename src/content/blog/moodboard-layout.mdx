## How to Create a Wedding Moodboard

Before booking florists or selecting linens, establish a cohesive aesthetic vision board to align your creative team.

### Steps to Build a Moodboard
1. **Define Core Aesthetics**: Select 3 descriptive words (e.g., *Modern, Romantic, Minimalist*).
2. **Curate Color Cards**: Pick 1 base tone, 2 supporting shades, and 2 metal/accent colors.
3. **Vendor Delivery**: Share your final PDF or Canva board with your florist and coordinator.

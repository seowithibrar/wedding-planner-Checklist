## Description
Plan your whole wedding in one beautiful, printable planner, from engagement to "I do".

This 54-page PDF keeps the money, the dates, the people and the day itself in one place. Print it at home, punch holes and keep it in a binder, or write on it in a PDF or note-taking app.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Our Wedding at a Glance and Our Vision pages
- Budget planner with typical category percentages
- Expense tracker, payment log and hidden-costs checklist
- 12-month planning timeline in 6 stages, with checklists and appointment logs
- Vendor booking order and contract checklist
- 10 vendor contact cards
- Venue comparison, venue tour questions and vendor comparison sheets
- Guest list for 130 guests, plus RSVP and meal summary
- Seating chart planner with 16 tables
- Wedding party and key people page
- Attire and beauty planner with fittings and trials
- Ceremony planner, reception run of show and music lists
- Photo shot list
- Wedding day timeline and day-of contacts with tip log
- Emergency kit checklist and final week schedule
- 12 undated monthly calendars
- Gift and thank-you tracker, and notes pages

DETAILS
- Instant digital download, 1 PDF file
- US Letter size (8.5 x 11 in), prints on A4 with "Fit to page"
- Undated, so you can start any month
- Soft blush and rose design that prints well in colour or black and white

## How to Choose the Perfect Wedding Venue

Your venue sets the tone, date, and budget framework for your entire celebration.

<PlannerQuote author="David Sterling" role="Venue Procurement Specialist">
Never sign a venue contract until you've walked the physical space at the exact time of day your ceremony will take place to check sun angles and acoustics.
</PlannerQuote>

<CalloutBox type="warning" title="Hidden Fee Alert">
A base quote of $10,000 can easily reach $14,000 after 22% service fees, 8% state tax, and mandatory security guards are factored in.
</CalloutBox>

### Essential Site Tour Questions
1. Is our date available? What are peak vs off-peak rates?
2. What is the exact guest capacity for seated dinner vs cocktail hour?
3. What is the Plan B rain backup space for outdoor ceremonies?
4. Are there noise restrictions or early music cutoffs?

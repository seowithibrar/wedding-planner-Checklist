Good to know

Questions families often ask.

For current dates and seat availability, contact admissions.

Which grades and programmes does KIMS offer?

KIMS offers Grades VI–VIII, O Level and A Level at its Gulberg II campus. Playgroup–Grade V is offered through Lahore School of Learning at a separate Gulberg III location.

How does the admission process work?

Submit the admission form and required documents. KIMS reviews prior results and may require an admission test. If an offer is made, admission is final once the fee is paid.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Contact KIMS to arrange a visit and confirm a suitable date and time.

Where can I check the latest fees and scholarships?

Read the published fee guide and scholarship page, then contact admissions to confirm the details for the current session.
